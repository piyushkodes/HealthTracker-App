# HealthTracker App — UI/UX Design

## Main Screens
- Dashboard: Overview of the user's health information.
- Health Records: View recorded health data.
- Add Record: Enter new health information.

## Design Principles
- Simple and clear navigation.
- Readable text and accessible controls.
- Consistent layout across screens.
- Clear validation and error messages.
- UI/UX review completed for HA-2
